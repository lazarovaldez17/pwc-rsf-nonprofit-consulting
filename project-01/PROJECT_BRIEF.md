# Reggie Stephens Foundation — Fundraising Strategy Externship

**Extern:** Lazaro Valdez
**Program:** PwC × Extern — Strategic Consulting Externship
**Client:** Reggie Stephens Foundation (RSF)
---

## 1. Project Overview

As a selected extern through the program hosted by PwC in partnership with Extern, over 8 weeks I will apply strategic consulting skills to analyze and evaluate challenges facing the Reggie Stephens Foundation. Using consulting frameworks, the goal is to help RSF solve high-priority problems across:

- Development (fundraising) process
- Impact assessment
- Policy / regulatory issues
- Process improvements
- Talent engagement
- Communication
- Strategic positioning

The arc of the work moves from **understanding how nonprofits raise money** → **diagnosing RSF's current position** → **generating actionable partnership leads** → **delivering recommendations** through research, communication, and AI-assisted workflow.

A foundational step is to **orient how fundraising has changed since the emergence of AI tools** — building a baseline of where the sector is headed *before* diving into analysis.

## 2. About the Client

### Mission
Provide access and opportunities — college access, leadership development, and economic mobility — for underserved young people with talent and drive, across four pillars: **Sports, Arts, Education, Culture.**

### ⚡ Priority Notice — Women's Flag Football
Women's flag football is an active and growing focus. RSF's affiliation with the **Golden State Storm** places it inside a space where funding is actively flowing right now. Women's sports investment is accelerating sector-wide, and RSF holds **early positioning that most comparable organizations don't.** This is a timing advantage to exploit, not a side note.

## 4. The Challenge

RSF faces significant fundraising challenges in a **competitive grant landscape** where many organizations chase the same funding sources.

- While Reggie has secured some grants, he **prefers partnership-based** (local businesses, sports brands) and **creative funding** (events, merch, campaigns) approaches over traditional grant-seeking, which is slow and restrictive.
- With limited resources, **Reggie often pays out of pocket** for student needs — jerseys, tournament fees, scholarships — which constrains RSF's ability to scale programs and expand scholarships.

---

## 5. Systematic & Strategic Approach

1. **Identify and cultivate aligned funders** who understand and value RSF's mission of youth empowerment through sports, education, and mentorship.
2. **Develop a strategic, efficient approach to donor identification and cultivation** that feels authentic to RSF's community-focused mission while leveraging modern tools to maximize impact.

---

## 6. Why It Matters

Securing sustainable funding is critical to RSF's ability to expand impact and serve more youth.

- **Seven students** are currently at Morgan State, with plans to add **seven more.**
- Growing **women's flag football** initiatives and **education partnerships** require reliable revenue streams to fund scholarships, program operations, and student support.
- Effective fundraising directly enables Reggie to **reduce out-of-pocket spending** and scale programs that have already placed students at top universities and created pathways for underserved youth.
- A strategic donor strategy strengthens RSF's partnerships with institutions like **Morgan State** and **Zenith Prep**, creating a **multiplier effect** for youth outcomes across the Central Coast region.

## 7. Project Timeline — Four Sub-Projects

The externship is divided into four sequential sub-projects, each with a defined deliverable.

### Sub-Project 1 — Establish the Context
Understand the foundation of RSF. Clarify what this specific team cares about, the constraints they operate under, and why the fundraising challenge matters.
**Deliverable:** Memo — the context articulated in my own words.

### Sub-Project 2 — Diagnose the Current Fundraising Landscape
Examine RSF's current fundraising approach and benchmark it against (a) comparable organizations and (b) emerging industry-wide AI adoption practices. In parallel, research where the nonprofit fundraising sector sits on its AI adoption curve, focused on practices relevant to small, lean teams.
**Deliverable:** Insights from comparative analysis and market research. *(Format TBD — see Open Questions.)*

### Sub-Project 3 — Generate Institutional Partnership Leads
Build and research a prioritized list of **5 institutional partnership prospects** for RSF using a defined shortlisting framework. The output is not a generic list — it's a ready-to-use lead set Reggie and Dominic can act on immediately, with enough context per lead that outreach can begin without further research.
**Deliverable:** 5 institutional partnership leads.

### Sub-Project 4 — Present Recommendations to RSF
Synthesize the entire externship journey into a practitioner-facing presentation and deliver recommendations to the RSF team. This phase is explicitly about **synthesis and clarity, not new research.**
**Deliverable:** Presentation deck + video presentation.
